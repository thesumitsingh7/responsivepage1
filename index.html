Our Philosophy

Rooted in Patience, Purity, and Fire

We reject industrial dough conditioners and commercial fast-rise yeasts. Authentic bread asks for only four elements: heritage flour, pure water, sea salt, and patient time.

01

Wild Fermentation

Our wild sourdough starter has been nurtured daily since 2018. Two full days of cold maturation break down complex gluten, unlocking rich lactic aromatics and effortless digestibility.

02

Stone-Milled Heritage Grain

We partner directly with regional regenerative grain farms. Whole-grain stone milling retains the living germ, natural wheat oils, and distinct terroir lost in industrial flour.

03

81-Layer Lamination

Every croissant and pastry is folded by hand at 16°C in our dedicated pastry kitchen. Cultured Normandy butter is laminated across 81 micro-layers for that signature crisp shatter.
